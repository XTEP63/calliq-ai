# Sources and claim boundaries for the CallIQ presentation

The deck presents a product proposal and a working MVP. It makes no invented ROI, accuracy, market-share, or customer adoption claims. The card replacement transcript and its provisional QA findings come from [`src/demo.py`](../src/demo.py): a synthetic script and manually authored demonstration judgment, not live model output or a real bank call.

## Presentation structure

- [Sequoia Capital, Writing a Business Plan](https://sequoiacap.com/article/writing-a-business-plan/): purpose, customer pain, solution, alternatives, and a clear ask.
- [Y Combinator, A Guide to Demo Day Presentations](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/): explain the product early, use one idea per slide, make visuals support the presenter, and avoid depending on a live demo.
- [Duarte, How to Make a Sales Presentation That Wins Deals](https://www.duarte.com/blog/sales-presentations-elevate-your-pitch-to-drive-success/): connect the story to a customer's situation and keep each slide focused.
